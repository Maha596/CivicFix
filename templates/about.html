About CivicFix System

Intelligent Public Infrastructure Issue Reporting & Resolution Platform

Project Objectives

  1. Provide citizens with a modern, intuitive platform to report civic infrastructure problems with photographic evidence.
  2. Integrate an AI-based Computer Vision classifier module to diagnose and categorize civic defects automatically.
  3. Utilize GPS and OpenStreetMap/Leaflet geospatial services to capture pinpoint defect locations.
  4. Formulate an explainable multi-criteria priority scoring engine evaluating inherent risk, reported severity, spatial density, and safety indicators.
  5. Equip municipal authorities with a centralized operations management dashboard featuring real-time analytics.
  6. Enforce transparent accountability by recording resolution photographic evidence before closing complaints.
  7. Detect and group duplicate submissions using the Haversine spatial proximity metric.

AI & Data Science Methodology

The CivicFix AI subsystem operates across three core data science disciplines:

1. Visual Feature Extraction & Image Classification

Extracts normalized color histograms, road-asphalt pixel ratios, vegetative foliage indices, specular liquid reflections, and spatial edge gradients. Features are scored against multi-class decision distributions and calibrated using softmax normalization to produce human-interpretable confidence ratings.

2. Spatial GIS & Haversine Deduplication

Evaluates great-circle distances between GPS coordinates using the Haversine formula to identify concurrent complaints within a 150-meter radius, linking duplicates without data loss.

3. Explainable Prioritization Algorithm

Calculates an urgency score (0-100) combining category hazard weights, citizen severity, neighborhood complaint clusters, hazard keywords, and temporal aging.
